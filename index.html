Why Kakeibo

Built around
how you actually think

No spreadsheets. No complicated dashboards. Just the clarity you need to make better financial decisions.

Four Pillars of Spending

Kakeibo organises every expense into Needs, Wants, Culture, and Savings — the four categories that define a balanced financial life.

Monthly Reflection

At the end of every month, Kakeibo asks the questions that matter — not just what you spent, but why, and what you'll do differently.

Visual Spending Rings

See your budget at a glance with colour-coded rings — no numbers to squint at, no tables to parse. Just instant clarity.

Savings Goals

Set goals, track progress, and celebrate milestones. Whether it's an emergency fund or a dream holiday — Kakeibo keeps you on track.

Your Data, Your Privacy

No selling your data. Your financial life is yours. Kakeibo stores only what you choose — nothing more.

50+ Currencies

Track in any currency worldwide. Whether you're in Lagos, London, or Dubai — Kakeibo speaks your financial language.
